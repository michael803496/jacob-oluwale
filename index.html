About

I am an accountant with a master’s degree in Public Sector Accounting. I have worked in accounting, audit and management since 2005, starting as a bookkeeper and rising to General Manager of Igboho (Oorelope) Ekolere NUT CICU Limited.

I work well with detail and can run a team or work alone with little supervision. My aim is to add value and strengthen the institutions I serve.

In 2024 I was elected Executive Chairman of Oorelope Local Government, where I lead the council and its revenue work.
